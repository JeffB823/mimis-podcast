## Mature women's health

1. I did not verify a major new menopause guideline, FDA action, or peer-reviewed menopause paper in the last 24 hours that cleared the show's bar.
2. Office on Women's Health guidance checked September 3, 2026 continues to identify hot flashes, sleep issues, and mood changes as common menopause or perimenopause symptoms. Practical consequence: when sleep or mood feels off, it does not need to be dismissed as "just getting older" or folded into vague detox language.
3. NCCIH's detoxes and cleanses page says there have been only a small number of studies on detoxification programs in people, that the studies have quality limitations, and that some programs can involve diarrhea, dehydration, malabsorption, or dangerous electrolyte imbalances. Evidence-safe framing to preserve: the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract already handle the body's normal waste-processing functions.
4. Evidence-safe small action for today: write down one symptom, one sleep or hydration note, and one question for a clinician or pharmacist before trying a cleanse or supplement. Encourage qualified medical care for symptoms that are persistent, severe, sudden, chest-related, bleeding-related, or otherwise concerning.
