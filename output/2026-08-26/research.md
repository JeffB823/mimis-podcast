## U.S. national news

1. Canada announced retaliatory tariffs on August 25, 2026 in response to the latest U.S. tariffs. Canada’s finance ministry said the countermeasures will apply to products covering C$27.6 billion in imports from the United States and will take effect September 8, 2026. AP reported the move as a new escalation in the U.S.-Canada trade fight. Practical consequence: this is the kind of trade action that can filter into prices, supply chains, and business uncertainty on the U.S. side, even before the tariffs actually start.
2. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration announced on August 25, 2026 that it authorized the Libre Duo 10 Day Continuous Dual Glucose Ketone Monitoring System for people age 2 and older with diabetes. FDA said it is the first wearable device in the United States to continuously monitor ketone levels and the first in the world to continuously monitor both ketones and blood sugar together in a single device. Practical consequence: this is a concrete medical-device approval with immediate relevance for diabetes care and emergency-risk monitoring.
3. The U.S. Department of Justice announced on August 25, 2026 that it had emergency-scheduled three highly potent opioid compounds: mitragynine pseudoindoxyl (MGPI), fluorofentanyl, and fluorocarfentanil. Practical consequence: this is a national public-safety signal about the drug supply, especially because DOJ framed the compounds as posing an imminent hazard.
