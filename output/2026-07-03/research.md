## U.S. national news

1. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics published the June 2026 Employment Situation on July 2, 2026. Confirmed topline: total nonfarm payroll employment increased by 57,000 in June and the unemployment rate was 4.2 percent. Employment continued to trend up in professional and business services, social assistance, and health care, while leisure and hospitality lost jobs. Practical consequence: this points to a labor market that is cooling rather than breaking, which matters for household caution going into the Independence Day weekend.
2. The U.S. Department of Labor's weekly unemployment-insurance release published July 2, 2026 said initial claims for the week ending June 27 were 215,000, down 1,000 from the prior week's revised level. Practical consequence: layoffs do not appear to be suddenly accelerating, even though monthly hiring remains modest.
3. The Federal Reserve said on July 1, 2026 that its 2025 triennial payments study found 236.6 billion noncash payments in 2024. Cards accounted for more than three quarters of noncash payments by number, and ACH accounted for nearly three quarters of noncash payments by value. Practical consequence: routine spending is increasingly digital and easy to overlook, which is useful framing for subscription and small-charge awareness.
