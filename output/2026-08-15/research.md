Practical consequence: the consumer side of the economy looked softer heading into the weekend, which matters because household spending has been doing much of the work of keeping overall growth going.
2. The University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers posted preliminary August 2026 results on August 14 showing its consumer sentiment index at 51.0, down from 55.2 in July. Practical consequence: households are not just facing high prices; they are sounding more uneasy again, which helps explain why softer retail sales are being taken seriously.
3. Practical consequence: this morning's economic picture is less "prices reaccelerating" and more "spending stamina may be fading."
