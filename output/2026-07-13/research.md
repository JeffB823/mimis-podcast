## Mature women's health

1. I did not verify a major new menopause or detox/cleanse evidence release in the last 24 hours that would change listener guidance for July 13, 2026.
2. NCCIH says evidence for detoxes and cleanses is limited and some approaches may be unsafe. The body normally processes wastes and byproducts through the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract.
3. The U.S. Office on Women's Health says menopause symptoms can include hot flashes, sleep problems, mood changes, and brain fog, and symptom burden can vary widely. Editorial consequence: on a hot, poor-sleep morning, it is safer to notice patterns first than to assume the body is "toxic" and needs a cleanse.
