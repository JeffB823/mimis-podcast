Practical consequence: weekend grocery and takeout choices still deserve one extra look.

## Mature women's health

1. I did not verify a major new menopause or detox/cleanse clinical development published in the last 24 hours that met the sourcing bar.
2. NCCIH guidance checked on August 22, 2026 says only a small number of studies have examined detoxes and cleanses and that the studies showing benefits were low quality. NCCIH also warns that some detoxes and cleanses can be unsafe, including dehydration and electrolyte problems.
3. Office on Women's Health pages checked on August 22, 2026 continue to treat hot flashes, sleep problems, and mood changes as real menopause symptoms and say treatment discussions should depend on how much symptoms are bothering daily life. OWH's treatment page says menopausal hormone therapy is prescription medicine that may help when symptoms are severe enough to disrupt daily life.
4. The Menopause Society press release dated July 14, 2026 summarized a study finding that 34% of U.S. women aged 35 and older reported uncertainty about whether they were in perimenopause. Practical consequence: uncertainty itself is common, and it is better framed as something to name and track than something to push away with a cleanse.
