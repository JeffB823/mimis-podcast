Confirmed fact: from October 7 to December 10, 2026, private cars will not be allowed to park all day at the Kyoto City Kiyomizuzaka tourist parking lot, and sightseeing buses will again require complete advance reservation. Editorial consequence: even in July, Kyoto is already tightening autumn traffic management around one of its most crowded areas.

Confirmed fact: buses without reservations will not be able to use the lot. Editorial consequence: this is a practical signal of how seriously the city is treating overtourism and road congestion.

Editorial consequence: this gives a local-life summer story that feels resident-facing, not only tourist-facing.

## Mature women's health

1. I did not verify a material new federal guidance document or major peer-reviewed menopause/detox development published in the last 24 hours that changes evidence-based advice for listeners this morning.
2. NCCIH says detoxes and cleanses have limited research support and that some approaches may be unsafe. Confirmed fact: normal waste processing is handled by the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract.
3. The U.S. Office on Women's Health says menopause treatment options include lifestyle changes, hormonal medicines, and nonhormonal medicines. The NIH-supported Women's Health Initiative says its findings continue to inform decisions about hormone therapy after menopause. Editorial consequence: the safest recurring message remains not to interpret fatigue, bloating, hot flashes, or poor sleep as proof that a cleanse is needed.
