## Kyoto

1. Kyoto City announced on July 9, 2026 that hand-foot-and-mouth disease reached warning level in the city. Confirmed details: for week 27, covering June 29 through July 5, reports per pediatric sentinel site rose to 10.11, above the warning benchmark of 5.0.
2. Kyoto City says hand-foot-and-mouth disease mainly spreads among infants and young children and again asked residents to reinforce handwashing and related infection-control habits. Practical consequence: this is especially relevant for grandparents and households moving between home, childcare, and summer outings.
3. Kyoto City also announced on July 8, 2026 the start of 「聞こえの応援事業」 for age-related hearing loss. Confirmed details: residents must be registered in Kyoto, be age 65 or older at purchase, attend a lecture, be judged by a cooperating doctor to need a hearing aid, buy from a designated seller, and apply from October 1, 2026. Practical consequence: this is a concrete aging-in-place support story with direct relevance for listeners in their 50s thinking ahead for themselves or parents.
