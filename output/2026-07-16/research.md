## Mature women's health

1. The Menopause Society published a press release dated July 14, 2026, and posted July 15, 2026 findings from a new study in its journal *Menopause* reporting that 34% of surveyed U.S. women age 35 and older were unsure of their reproductive stage; that uncertainty rose to 42% among ages 40-44 and 37% among those with severe symptom burden. Practical consequence: confusion in the menopause transition is common, not a personal failure.
2. The U.S. Office on Women's Health menopause basics page, accessed July 16, 2026, continues to describe menopause as a normal life stage and notes that symptoms can include hot flashes, sleep problems, and brain fog. Practical consequence: symptoms that disturb sleep or concentration should not automatically be dismissed as laziness, lack of discipline, or "just stress."
3. NCCIH's detox-and-cleanse guidance, accessed July 16, 2026, says evidence for detoxes and cleanses is limited and some approaches may be unsafe. The body normally processes wastes and byproducts through the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract. Practical consequence: it is safer to track symptoms and discuss persistent or concerning changes with a qualified clinician than to jump to a cleanse.
