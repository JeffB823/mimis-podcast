## Kyoto

1. Kyoto City announced on July 8, 2026 the start of 「聞こえの応援事業」, a subsidy program for hearing-aid purchases tied to age-related hearing loss. Confirmed fact: the city says applicants must be Kyoto residents, age 65 or older at purchase, not hold a hearing-disability certificate, attend a city-linked lecture, be judged by a cooperating doctor to need a hearing aid, buy from a designated seller, and participate in a city exercise or prevention program. Confirmed date: applications open October 1, 2026. Practical consequence: this is a concrete midlife-and-older support policy, not just a general wellness message.
2. Kyoto City also announced on July 8, 2026 a social experiment to expand support for older residents and others who have difficulty taking trash to collection points. Confirmed fact: the city says applications run from July 15 through August 31, 2026, the trial runs from October 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027, and the expansion would loosen eligibility and add small appliances, used clothing, and batteries to the pickup list in selected school districts. Practical consequence: this is a highly practical local-life and aging-in-place story.
3. These July 8 city releases are stronger fresh material for today's episode than carrying forward yesterday's Kiyomizuzaka traffic announcement or zoo night-opening notice without a new development.
