Practical consequence: Kyoto’s support has moved further from symbolic dispatch into sustained infrastructure recovery.

Practical consequence: support is being matched to the timeline of lived recovery, not just the shock phase.

Practical consequence: this is a local-life story about neighborhood landscape and seasonal vigilance.
