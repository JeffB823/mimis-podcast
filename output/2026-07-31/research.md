3. NCCIH also continues to warn that some detox or fasting-style approaches can cause harms such as weakness, dehydration, fainting, and nutritional problems depending on what is being attempted.
4. The Office on Women's Health continues to advise that menopause symptoms that disrupt daily life are appropriate to discuss with a health care professional, and The Menopause Society continues to offer plain-language patient education on symptom patterns and tracking.
5. Practical consequence: today's evidence-safe action should stay modest. A good choice is to write down one symptom, when it tends to show up, and one question to raise with a clinician if that symptom is persisting, worsening, sudden, or concerning.
