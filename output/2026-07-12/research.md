## Mature women's health

2. NCCIH says the evidence for detoxes and cleanses is limited and some approaches may be unsafe. The body’s normal waste-processing functions are handled through the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract.
3. FDA's weight-loss product notifications page warns that many products marketed for weight loss, including pills, teas, and supplements, may contain dangerous hidden ingredients and may be falsely marketed as natural treatments. Editorial consequence: for listeners feeling sluggish or puffy, the safest useful move is not to “flush” the body but to pause before buying a product, check what it contains, and talk with a clinician or pharmacist if symptoms are persistent or a product could interact with medications.
