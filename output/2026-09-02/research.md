## Mature women's health

1. I did not verify a major new menopause guideline, FDA action, or peer-reviewed menopause paper in the last 24 hours that cleared the show's bar.
2. Office on Women's Health guidance checked September 2, 2026 continues to describe hot flashes, sleep problems, and mood changes as common menopause or perimenopause symptoms. Practical consequence: vague discomfort or lighter sleep does not have to be dismissed as imagination or "just stress."
3. NCCIH's detoxes and cleanses fact sheet still says that only a small number of studies of detoxes and cleanses have been conducted in people and that some programs can cause diarrhea, dehydration, and electrolyte imbalances. Evidence-safe framing to preserve: the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract already perform the body's normal waste-processing functions.
4. Evidence-safe small action for today: before trying a cleanse, write down one symptom, one meal or hydration note, and one question for a clinician or pharmacist. Encourage qualified medical care for symptoms that are persistent, severe, sudden, chest-related, bleeding-related, or otherwise concerning.
