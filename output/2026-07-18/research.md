## Mature women's health

1. The Menopause Society's July 14, 2026 press release, still current in this editorial window, says roughly one-third of women surveyed age 35 and older were unsure of their reproductive stage. Practical consequence: confusion in perimenopause is common and should not be framed as a personal failure.
2. ACOG's 2026 updated guidance on evaluation of postmenopausal bleeding says most patients should have both transvaginal ultrasonography and endometrial tissue sampling at initial evaluation. Practical consequence: bleeding after menopause is not something to dismiss or to cover over with self-treatment.
3. NCCIH's detox-and-cleanse guidance, accessed July 18, 2026, says evidence for detoxes and cleanses is limited and some approaches may be unsafe. The body normally processes wastes and byproducts through the liver, kidneys, lungs, skin, and gastrointestinal tract. Practical consequence: for midlife symptoms, tracking changes and seeking qualified care is safer than assuming a cleanse will fix the problem.
