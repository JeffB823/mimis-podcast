## U.S. national news

1. The White House published an executive order on August 26, 2026 declaring a national emergency related to the United States bulk-power system. The order says certain foreign-produced bulk-power equipment can be prohibited when the Secretary of Energy determines it poses an unacceptable risk to national security or critical infrastructure. Practical consequence: this is an electricity-grid and supply-chain policy story, not just a Washington headline, because it can affect how utilities and large infrastructure operators source equipment.
2. The Justice Department and FBI announced on August 26, 2026 that they had seized two platforms, QScan and QTRouter, used by a China state-sponsored hacking group to target U.S. critical infrastructure and sensitive networks. DOJ said named victims included NASA, the Federal Reserve, the Department of Energy, DOJ, HHS, NIH, and the U.S. Senate. Practical consequence: this is a reminder that cyber risk is not abstract; it touches services and institutions Americans rely on every day.
3. The FDA announced on August 26, 2026 that it approved Rasonque (daraxonrasib), describing it as a first-in-class targeted therapy for the most common form of metastatic pancreatic cancer. Practical consequence: this is not a mass-market consumer story, but it is a concrete example of a meaningful cancer-treatment approval with relevance for patients and families watching serious illness closely.
